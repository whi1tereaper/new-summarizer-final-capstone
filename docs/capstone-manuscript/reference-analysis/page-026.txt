management has shifted from a clerical support
function to a strategic system for efficiency,
accountability, and compliance in both public
and  private  organizations  (Anike,  2025;
Savchenko et al., 2024). Contemporary          digital
records systems are designed not only to store
documents, but also to support searchability,
secure sharing, auditability, and long-term
accessibility across the records life cycle
