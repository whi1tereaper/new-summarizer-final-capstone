                       Abstract

    This study presents the design, development,
and evaluation of AccredX: A Record Management
and  Archiving  System  for  the  Nueva  Ecija
University of Science and Technology (NEUST)
Peñaranda   Off-Campus.   The   institution's
existing record-keeping practices, which rely
heavily on manual processes and unstructured
Google Drive storage, have led to chronic issues
including misplaced files, delayed document
retrieval, inadequate security controls, and
significant operational inefficiencies during
accreditation   periods.   These   persistent
challenges underscore the critical need for a
structured and dedicated digital solution.

    AccredX  was  developed  following  the
Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) using the
Agile methodology, which allowed for iterative
refinement  based  on  continuous  stakeholder
feedback.   The   system   integrates   key
functionalities including centralized document
storage, advanced search and filtering, folder
and file management, version control, role-
based   access   control,   audit   trails,
accreditation folder checklists, and compliance
report generation. These features collectively
address the document organization, security,


                                                      xvii


===== PDF PAGE 18 =====
and   accessibility   requirements   of   the
institution.

    The system was evaluated by two groups of
respondents:  ten  (10)  IT  consultants  who
assessed technical quality based on ISO/IEC
25010:2011 standards, and twenty-three (23)
end-users composed of administrative staff,
teachers, and assistants. IT consultants          rated
the system with an overall weighted mean of 3.38
(Very Satisfactory), with strong performance
across  all  criteria  including  functional
suitability, security, performance efficiency,
compatibility,  usability,  reliability,  and
portability.  End-users  similarly  gave  the
system an overall weighted mean of 3.49 (Very
Satisfactory), with particularly high marks in
usability,   functional   correctness,   and
performance efficiency.

    The   findings   confirm   that   AccredX
effectively    addresses    the    documented
shortcomings of the institution's prior record
management  approach.  The  system  enhances
document   integrity,   strengthens   security
through controlled access, and significantly
improves  the  institution's  readiness  for
accreditation. This study contributes to a



                                                     xviii


===== PDF PAGE 19 =====
scalable and practical model for digital records
management  that  can  be  adopted  by  other
educational   institutions   facing   similar
challenges.

                      Chapter I
          THE PROBLEM AND ITS BACKGROUND

Introduction

  In  today's  digital  age,  proper  document
management and archiving are critical to the
efficiency,  security,  and  compliance  of
organizational operations. Records management
is the systematic control of records from
creation to disposal, ensuring that critical
information is available, protected, and kept
(Dearstyne, 2020; Bailey, 2021).

 As technology has advanced, organizations have
switched from conventional manual procedures to
electronic    records    management    systems
(ERMS), that automate classification, retrieval,
and storage processes. These modern systems help
reduce manual effort, improve security through
encryption and access control, and support
compliance   with   legal   and   regulatory
requirements (Smith & Johnson, 2020; Patterson,
2022).

 According  to  trends,  digital  archiving
solutions are especially beneficial to the
education  industry.  Academic  institutions
manage massive amounts of crucial papers, and



                                                       1


===== PDF PAGE 21 =====
the necessity for secure, organized, and easily
available data is especially important during
accreditation processes (Mitchell & Carter,
2020).   Implementing   a   reliable   record
management system ensures that accreditation
documents are well-organized, secure from data
loss,  and  easily  accessible  for  review.
According to studies, organizations that use
cloud-based archiving benefit from increased
collaboration, faster information retrieval,
and lower infrastructure expenses (Harris, 2021;
Thomassen, 2021).     However, challenges remain,
particularly in smaller academic institutions
like Nueva Ecija University of Science and
Technology   (NEUST)   Peñaranda   Off-Campus.
Current manual or semi-digital record-keeping
processes have resulted in inefficiencies such
as missing data, retrieval delays, and security
issues. These issues not only affect           everyday
administrative operations, but also compromise
the     institution's     preparedness     for
accreditation and review processes.

  This study aims to create a Record Management
and Archiving System for NEUST Peñaranda Off-
Campus. The system will replace manual or semi-
digital processes, improve document retrieval,
storage, and organization, and strengthen the

  This study makes a valuable contribution by

Review of Related Literature

   Record management and archiving are essential
for maintaining organizational efficiency and
compliance with regulations. Records management
involves the systematic control of records
throughout their life-cycle. Effective record
management ensures compliance, security, and
accessibility    (Dearstyne,    2020).    The
development of records management and archiving
systems has significantly evolved with the
advancement of digital technology. A well-
organized   records   management   system   is
essential for businesses and         institutions to
store, retrieve, and secure essential documents
efficiently. According to Bailey (2021), modern
electronic records management systems (ERMS)
enable   organizations   to   automate   the
classification,  storage,  and  retrieval  of
records, reducing manual effort and improving
document security.
   Records  management  and  archiving  systems
ensure compliance with legal and regulatory
requirements. As stated by Smith & Johnson
(2020), organizations that implement electronic
records  management  can  improve  regulatory
compliance by automatically tracking document
retention schedules and ensuring that sensitive
records are disposed of properly. Additionally,


                                                       4


===== PDF PAGE 24 =====
automated archiving reduces the risk of document
misplacement and enhances information retrieval
speed.    Digital  archives  ensure  long-term
preservation   and   retrieval   of   critical
documents (Thomassen, 2021).
   Digital records management also enhances data
security  and  accessibility.  According  to
Patterson   (2022),   cloud-based   archiving
solutions provide organizations with encrypted
storage and role-based access, ensuring that
only authorized personnel can access sensitive
records. Furthermore, implementing a structured
digital archiving system minimizes data loss and
ensures disaster recovery (Thompson & Lee, 2020).
Studies indicate that organizations using cloud
storage experience improved collaboration and
reduce infrastructure costs (Harris, 2021). In
the educational sector, a study by Mitchell &
Carter   (2020)   highlighted   that   digital
archiving systems help academic institutions
manage student records efficiently, ensuring
long-term   storage   and   easy   retrieval.
Additionally,   digital   solutions   prevent
unauthorized   alterations,   ensuring   data
integrity.   Encryption,   role-based   access
control (RBAC), and audit trails are crucial for
protecting sensitive records (Anderson, 2022).




                                                        5


===== PDF PAGE 25 =====
   The adoption of standardized frameworks and
best practices in records management further
improves efficiency and compliance. According
to Wilson (2021), implementing international
standards  such  as  ISO  15489  for  records
management  ensures  systematic  control  over
document creation, maintenance, and disposal.
Proper  indexing  and  metadata  tagging  also
enhance  the  retrieval  process,  allowing
organizations to locate records quickly and
accurately (Miller & Adams, 2022). Additionally,
digitization of paper records reduces physical
storage  requirements  and  mitigates  risks
associated with document deterioration or loss
(Clark, 2020). Effective training programs for
staff  on  records  management  policies  and
procedures  are  essential  in  maintaining
consistency  and  preventing  mishandling  of
sensitive   information   (Henderson,   2021).
Moreover, backup strategies, such as redundant
storage and offsite archiving, play a crucial
role  in  disaster  recovery  and  business
continuity (Turner & Scott, 2022).
   The  integration  of  electronic  document
management systems (EDMS) has further enhanced
the efficiency of records management. According
to Ramirez (2021), EDMS allows organizations to
store, categorize, and retrieve records with



                                                       6


===== PDF PAGE 26 =====
greater accuracy and speed. The use of version
control mechanisms ensures that the latest
document  revisions  are  accessible  while
preserving historical records for reference
(Collins   &   Hayes,   2020).   Additionally,
implementing  automated  retention  schedules
helps  organizations  comply  with  legal  and
regulatory  requirements  by  systematically
disposing of outdated records (Stewart, 2022).
Regular system maintenance and data migration
strategies are also essential to ensure long-
term accessibility and prevent data corruption
or loss (Foster, 2021).

   Recent  studies  reinforce  that  records
management has shifted from a clerical support
function to a strategic system for efficiency,
accountability, and compliance in both public
and  private  organizations  (Anike,  2025;
Savchenko et al., 2024). Contemporary          digital
records systems are designed not only to store
documents, but also to support searchability,
secure sharing, auditability, and long-term
accessibility across the records life cycle
(Qaddoumi, 2025; Anike, 2025; Benmakhlouf &
Chouaou, 2024). This broader role is especially
important as institutions manage increasing
document  volumes  and  face  stronger  legal,



                                                       7


===== PDF PAGE 27 =====
operational,  and  transparency  requirements
(Chode,  2025;  Savchenko  et  al.,  2024;
Benmakhlouf & Chouaou, 2024).
   Across sectors, digital archiving systems
consistently  report  better retrieval  speed,
storage    efficiency,    and    operational
control than manual or paper-based approaches
(Yulianti et al., 2025; Erin et al., 2025;
Pratiwi et al., 2025). Manual systems are
repeatedly  associated  with  slow  retrieval,
misplaced  files,  redundancy,  damaged  paper
records,  and  higher  administrative  burden
(Justina et al., 2022; Kustiningsih et al.,
2026).  Electronic  archivation  also  reduces
physical storage needs and can cut storage costs
substantially  through  automation  and  cloud
technologies,   with   one   recent   study
estimating 40–60% lower storage costs (Moaiyad,
2025).  In  addition,  centralized  digital
repositories support faster decision-making,
smoother workflows, and better continuity after
disruptive events such as fires or floods
(Savchenko et al., 2024; Benmakhlouf & Chouaou,
2024; Kustiningsih et al., 2026).
   Recent literature also emphasizes that secure
digital archiving depends on access control,
encryption, backup, and audit functions rather
than simple file storage alone (Qaddoumi, 2025;



                                                       8


===== PDF PAGE 28 =====
Yulianti et al., 2025; Pratiwi et al., 2025).
Cloud-based document systems are used because
they allow unified upload, download, sharing,
auditing, retrieval, and role setting while
improving scalability and reducing operational
costs (Han et al., 2021; Sipahutar et al., 2025).
A secured cloud-based EDMS for a university
records  unit  used AES  encryption and  was
reported to be effective in storing, retrieving,
and sharing documents securely (Justina et al.,
2022). More recent intelligent document systems
extend these features with OCR, semantic search,
automated metadata extraction, audit logging,
and  policy-based  retention  to  strengthen
compliance readiness and reduce manual handling
time (Chode, 2025).
   In educational institutions, the evidence
especially supports ERMS adoption for academic
records, archives, and accountability (Oktarina
et al., 2025; Jannah et al., 2023; Suripto et
al., 2025). Digital systems in schools and
universities improve document          accessibility,
retrieval speed, and monitoring, while also
producing  clearer  audit  trails  and  more
transparent administration (Oktarina et al.,
2025;  Suripto  et  al.,  2025).  One  higher-
education accountability study found that most
archivists using ERMS could retrieve documents



                                                       9


===== PDF PAGE 29 =====
in less  than  one  minute,  while  document
availability and completeness remained high
across education standards (Oktarina et al.,
2025).  A  registrar  archiving  system  also
received excellent user ratings for accuracy,
user-friendliness,  reliability,  operability,
and cross-platform performance (Mallares et al.,
2024). These findings indicate that digital
archiving in education supports both daily
administrative  efficiency  and  compliance-
oriented documentation.
   The recent literature is equally clear that
implementation problems remain. Common barriers
include high installation costs, infrastructure
limitations, weak policies, legal requirements,
staff     resistance,     and     inadequate
training (Jannah et al., 2023; Suripto et al.,
2025). Reviews of higher education records
management also identify lack of digital skills,
resource constraints, poor strategic alignment,
and  security  risks  as  major  obstacles  to
effective adoption (P. & E., 2025). Several
studies  therefore  recommend  formal  SOPs,
continuing   staff   development,   stronger
managerial support, periodic audits, and phased
infrastructure improvement to sustain system
use and compliance (Yulianti et al., 2025;
Oktarina et al., 2025; Suripto et al., 2025).

   The research design serves as a structured

              RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
   This  chapter  explains  the  analysis  and

identified in the study’s Statement of the

   1. Problems Encountered in the Existing
      System.
      The existing system of record management
at NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus still relies
heavily on manual and semi-digital processes
that make document organization, retrieval, and
archiving inefficient.       While Google Drive is
used to store and share files, it lacks a
centralized structure, proper categorization
for academic and administrative records, and a
checklist  feature  for  tracking  required
documents or completion status. This results in
difficulties in locating specific documents and
monitoring file requirements when needed. File
duplication, inconsistent naming conventions,
and the absence of version control further
complicate the process. Moreover, since Google
Drive  access  depends  on  individual  user
management,  data  security  and  control  are
limited, increasing the risk of          unauthorized


                                                      35


===== PDF PAGE 55 =====
access or accidental deletion. There is also no
automated tracking or monitoring of uploaded
files,   making   it   challenging   for   the
administration to ensure accountability and
proper record maintenance. These inefficiencies
highlight the need for a more structured, secure,
and dedicated Record Management and Archiving
System   tailored   to   the   institution’s
operational needs.

2. How AccredX Improves File Retrieval, Security,
   and Efficiency in Accreditation Processes
The AccredX system addresses the inefficiencies
of the existing record management practices at
NEUST  Peñaranda  Off-Campus  by  introducing
features that directly enhance file retrieval,
security, and overall efficiency, particularly
for accreditation processes.

2.1 Improvement in File Retrieval
The   system   significantly   improves   file
retrieval through the following mechanisms:

   •  Advanced Search and Filtering: Users can
      quickly locate documents using keywords,
      file  names,  date  ranges,  or  folder
      locations.  This  eliminates  the  time-
      consuming  manual  searching  previously





                                                        36


===== PDF PAGE 56 =====
      required   when   files   were   stored
      unstructured in Google Drive.

   •  Centralized       Folder       Structure:
      Accreditation documents are organized by
      Area (e.g., Area I      –  Vision and Mission,
      Area II   –  Faculty, etc.), allowing users
      to  navigate  directly  to  the  relevant
      section without guesswork.

   •  Version Control: Users can access previous
      versions of a document, ensuring that
      historical  records  are  not  lost  when
      updates are made. This is particularly
      valuable  during  accreditation,  where
      earlier drafts or supporting documents may
      be required for review.

   •  Folder Checklist Feature: Each folder can
      have an associated checklist of required
      documents. Users can immediately see which
      files have been uploaded and which are
      still pending, reducing the time spent
      verifying completeness.

   •  Sort & Filter Options: Items can be sorted
      by folders only, files only, marked, or
      unmarked,  enabling  users  to  focus  on
      specific  document  types  or  pending
      requirements.
These features collectively reduce retrieval
time from minutes or hours to seconds, directly



                                                       37


===== PDF PAGE 57 =====
supporting   the   efficiency   demands   of
accreditation preparation.

2.2 Improvement in Security
The system enhances document security through
multiple layers of control:

   •  Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Users
      are  assigned  roles  (Admin,  Teacher,
      Assistant) that determine which folders
      and files they can access. Teachers and
      assistants  (Compliance  Managers)  are
      limited to the specific Areas assigned to
      them by the Admin, preventing unauthorized
      viewing  or  modification  of  sensitive
      records.

   •  User Authentication: Secure login with
      email  and  password  ensures  that  only
      registered users can access the system. The
      password reset feature adds an additional
      layer of account recovery security.

   •  Audit Trails: The system tracks who created,
      viewed, modified, archived, or deleted
      each file. This accountability ensures
      that   any   changes   to   accreditation
      documents are logged and traceable.

   3. Essential Features and Functionalities of
      an  Effective  Record  Management  and
      Archiving System
   Based  on  the  needs  identified  at  NEUST
   Peñaranda  Off-Campus  and  the  features



                                                       40


===== PDF PAGE 60 =====
   implemented in AccredX, the following are the
   essential   features   and   functionalities
   required for an effective Record Management
   and Archiving System.

   3.1 Core Features Identified as Essential

   A truly effective record management system
   must  first  provide  centralized  document
   storage, creating a single digital repository
   where all records are kept. This eliminates
   scattered file locations and establishes a
   single source of truth for the institution.
   Closely related is folder and file management,
   which allows users to create, rename, move,
   and  delete  folders  and  files,  enabling
   logical organization of records by category
   such as accreditation Areas.

   To address retrieval delays, the system must
   include  advanced  search  and  filtering
   capabilities, including keyword search, date
   filters, file type filters, and sort options.
   This feature significantly reduces the time
   spent locating specific documents. Document
   version control is another critical feature,
   as it tracks file versions and allows users
   to  access  previous  versions,  preserving



                                                       41


===== PDF PAGE 61 =====
   document history and preventing data loss
   from updates.

   For security and proper access, role-based
   access control (RBAC) is essential. This
   provides different permission levels based on
   user  roles  such  as  Admin,  Teacher,  or
   Assistant, ensuring document security and
   preventing       unauthorized       access.
   Complementing this is user account management,
   which gives administrators the ability to
   create, enable, disable, and archive user
   accounts, maintaining control over who can
   access the system.

   Accountability is supported through audit
   trails, which log all user actions including
   create,  view,  edit,  delete,  and  archive
   activities. This provides transparency and
   supports   compliance   reviews.   Checklist
   management  is  particularly  important  for
   accreditation, as it allows the creation of
   required document checklists within folders,
   tracking completeness of requirements in real
   time.

   The system must also include archiving and
   retention features, enabling users to archive



                                                       42


===== PDF PAGE 62 =====
   old records and recover deleted items with a
   retention  period  (e.g.,  90  days  before
   permanent deletion). This supports long-term
   preservation and prevents accidental data
   loss. Compliance reporting is another vital
   function,  generating  reports  that  show
   document status, completion percentages, and
   accreditation readiness, directly supporting
   decision-making      and      accreditation
   preparation.

   A dashboard with analytics provides at-a-
   glance   visibility   into   system   and
   accreditation   status,   displaying   total
   folders,  total  files,  archived  items,
   compliance status, and pending documents.
   File upload with conflict resolution (options
   to cancel, replace, or keep both files when
   uploading  duplicates)  prevents  accidental
   overwriting and preserves file integrity.
   Finally, user profile management allows users
   to update profile pictures, email addresses,
   and passwords, supporting personalization and
   account security.







                                                       43


===== PDF PAGE 63 =====
   3.2 Security Features Identified as Essential

   Beyond the core features, several security-
   specific functionalities are essential. The
   system must implement secure authentication
   using email and password-based login with
   password reset functionality. Account status
   control enables administrators to enable or
   disable user accounts as needed. Assigned
   folder restrictions limit user access to only
   the folders assigned to them, preventing
   unauthorized viewing of sensitive records.
   Audit logging records all user activities for
   accountability, and a bin and recovery system
   with a 90-day retention period ensures that
   accidentally deleted items can be restored
   before permanent removal.

   3.3 User Experience Features Identified as
   Essential

   To ensure the system is actually adopted and
   used effectively, certain user experience
   features are necessary. The interface should
   provide intuitive navigation with a clear
   folder hierarchy and easy-to-locate action
   buttons. Progress indicators such as upload
   progress bars give users visual feedback



                                                      44


===== PDF PAGE 64 =====
   during file operations. Clear error handling
   with  user-friendly  messages  helps  users
   understand  and  resolve  issues  such  as
   duplicate file uploads. Finally, the system
   must prioritize accessibility, ensuring that
   staff  with  varying  levels  of  technical
   proficiency can use it without difficulty.

   These   essential   features   formed   the
   foundation of the AccredX system and directly
   address the challenges identified in the
   existing record management practices at NEUST
   Peñaranda Off-Campus.

                      Chapter IV
    SUMMARY, CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Summary
   This study aimed to develop AccredX: Record
Management  and  Archiving  System  for  NEUST
Peñaranda  Off-Campus  in  response  to  the
institution’s need for a more efficient, secure,
and organized method of managing academic and
administrative documents. The existing manual
and semi-digital processes, including reliance
on    Google    Drive    without    structured
categorization or checklist tracking, resulted
in misplaced files, retrieval delays, limited
security measures, and inefficiencies during
accreditation.

   To address these concerns, the researchers
designed and developed a digital system using
the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) under
the Agile methodology. The system integrated
essential features such as         advanced   search    &
filtering    and  retrieval,  folder  and  file
management,  version  control,  audit  trails,
role-based   access   control,   accreditation
checklists, and report generation. Visual tools
including Use Case Diagrams, Data Flow Diagrams
(DFD), Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD), and



                                                      104


===== PDF PAGE 124 =====
Database  Normalization  guided  the  system’s
structure and functionality.

   Two groups evaluated the system: ten (10) IT
consultants assessing technical quality based
on ISO/IEC 25010:2011 and twenty-three (23) end-
users   evaluating   usability   and   actual
functionality in an academic context. Both
groups rated the system highly across all
criteria.


   IT consultants rated the system as Excellent
overall (WM = 3.38), with high ratings in
functional suitability, performance efficiency,
compatibility, usability, reliability, security,
and portability. End users likewise rated the
system  Excellent  overall  (WM  =  3.49),
highlighting  its  ease  of  use,  efficient
performance, and completeness of features.

   The study successfully delivered a functional,
secure, and user-friendly record management
system capable of supporting NEUST Peñaranda
Off-Campus   in   improving   administrative
processes, ensuring document integrity, and
strengthening accreditation preparation.

Conclusion
  Based on the findings of the study, the
following conclusions were drawn:

     1.  The existing record management system
  at NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus was inadequate
  for  the  institution's  operational  and
  accreditation needs. The reliance on Google
  Drive without structured organization, proper
  categorization, checklist functionality, or
  centralized  control  created  significant
  inefficiencies,    security    risks,    and
  difficulties  in  document  retrieval  and
  accountability.

     2.      The Agile methodology and SDLC
  framework proved effective        in developing a
  comprehensive    and    responsive    record
  management system. The iterative development
  approach allowed for continuous refinement
  based on stakeholder feedback, ensuring the
  final   product   aligned   closely   with
  institutional    requirements    and    user
  expectations.
     3.     AccredX successfully addresses the
  identified     problems            through     its
  implementation   of   essential   features



                                                     106


===== PDF PAGE 126 =====
   including  centralized  storage,  role-based
   access control, audit trails, version control,
   and accreditation checklist management. These
   functionalities     collectively     enhance
   document       organization,       security,
   accessibility, and accountability.

      4.  The system demonstrates high technical
   quality    as  evidenced  by  IT  consultants'
   ratings  across  all  ISO/IEC  25010:2011
   criteria. Particularly strong performance in
   functional   suitability,   security,   and
   performance  efficiency  confirms  that  the
   system    meets    professional    software
   development  standards  and        is  technically
   sound.

      5.     End-users find the system highly
   functional and usable      in actual operational
   contexts. The consistently high ratings from
   administrative    staff,    teachers,    and
   assistants  indicate  that  the  system  is
   intuitive, efficient, and well-suited to the
   daily tasks and workflows of its intended
   users.

      6.     The system significantly improves
   accreditation   preparedness          by   ensuring

Recommendations
   The conclusions and findings of the study
have led to the formulation of the following
recommendations:

   1. Implement Additional Security Enhancements
      Although the system already meets high
      security standards, integrating advanced
      features such as two-factor authentication
      (2FA), IP logging, and automated security
      alerts can further strengthen protection
      against  unauthorized  access  and  data
      breaches.

   2. Develop a Mobile-Friendly or Mobile App
      Version
      Creating a responsive mobile layout or a
      dedicated mobile application would allow
      users—especially  teachers  and  staff—to



                                                      108


===== PDF PAGE 128 =====
      access   records   conveniently   using
      smartphones or tablets, improving overall
      accessibility.


   3. Integrate  Automated  Backups  and  Cloud
      Storage Options
      To ensure long-term data preservation, the
      system should implement automated backup
      schedules. Cloud-based backup integration
      may also be considered to prevent data loss
      in  the  event  of  system  failures  or
      disasters.


   4. Provide Comprehensive User Training and
      Documentation
      Conducting  regular  training  sessions,
      creating  user  manuals,  and  producing
      tutorial videos would help ensure that all
      users—teachers,      assistants,      and
      administrators—maximize   the   system’s
      features effectively.


   5. Conduct Continuous System Maintenance and
      Updates
      Regular updates are recommended to fix
      emerging bugs, improve the user interface,



                                                      109


===== PDF PAGE 129 =====
      enhance performance, and incorporate new
      features based on user feedback.

   6. Explore  Integration        with  Other  NEUST
      Systems
      Future studies may focus on integrating the
      system    with    other    academic    or
      administrative platforms within NEUST to
      create a more unified and streamlined
      workflow across the campus.


   7. Expand   the   Checklist   Feature   for
      Accreditation
      Enhancing the checklist module by adding
      automated       reminders,       due-date
      notifications, and compliance percentage
      tracking     will     further     improve
      accreditation    readiness    and    user
      accountability.

   8. Conduct   Further   Studies   and   User
      Experience Testing
      Researchers   may   conduct   additional
      evaluations, usability testing sessions,
      and longitudinal studies to assess long-
      term user satisfaction, system reliability,
      and its overall impact on institutional
      efficiency.

                                  Appendix G.
                    AccredX Maintenance Manual

                AccredX Maintenance Manual

1. Introduction
AccredX is a web-based Record Management and Archiving System developed to
streamline accreditation document management. This manual provides
administrators and future developers with guidance for operating, maintaining,
updating, and troubleshooting the system. It serves as a reference throughout the
software lifecycle.

2. System Overview
AccredX centralizes accreditation files into organized folders, parameters, and
modules. It supports secure uploads, version control, document preview, search,
reporting, archive, recycle bin, and RET modules. The objective is to reduce
manual document handling while improving accessibility, integrity, and security.

3. Major Features
•   User authentication and role-based access.
•   Dashboard with statistics and recent activity.
•   Document upload with metadata.
•   Folder organization.
•   Document preview and download.
•   Version control for replacing or keeping previous revisions.
•   Archive and recycle bin.
•   Advanced search including extracted text.
•   RET (Research, Extension, Training) management.
•   Reports and activity monitoring.

4. System Requirements

Hardware
•   Computer (Desktop or Laptop)
•   8 GB RAM recommended
•   SSD

                                                                                       142


===== PDF PAGE 162 =====
•   Stable Internet Connection

Software
•   Windows 10/11
•   Apache/XAMPP
•   PHP
•   MySQL
•   Modern browser


5. Preventive Maintenance Procedures

Database Maintenance
•   Perform daily backups.
•   Optimize tables monthly.
•   Verify integrity after recovery.
•   Remove obsolete temporary records.

File Storage
•   Monitor disk usage.
•   Check upload folders.
•   Verify permissions.
•   Scan uploaded files.

Security
•   Change admin passwords periodically.
•   Review user permissions.
•   Enable HTTPS in production.
•   Update dependencies.

Performance
•   Review logs.
•   Monitor response times.
•   Clear cache.
•   Test large uploads.

Backup and Recovery
•   Maintain daily and weekly backups.


                                                                                   143


===== PDF PAGE 163 =====
•    Store offsite copies.
•    Test restoration quarterly.

6. Troubleshooting Guide
 Problem                        Possible Cause                     Solution
 Cannot login                   Incorrect                          Reset password and
                                credentials/database issue         verify DB connection.
 Upload fails                   File size or permissions           Increase limits and
                                                                   verify folder
                                                                   permissions.
 Preview unavailable            Unsupported/corrupt file           Re-upload or verify
                                                                   preview service.
 Slow system                    Large database                     Optimize DB and
                                                                   archive old records.
 Search incomplete              Index outdated                     Rebuild extracted text
                                                                   index.

7. Roles and Responsibilities
System Administrator: manages users, backups, updates, security, monitoring.
Staff: upload, organize, and maintain document quality.
Developers: maintain source code, fix bugs, implement enhancements.

8. Data Security and Privacy
Follow least-privilege access, maintain audit logs, encrypt credentials, perform
regular backups, and comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

9. Maintenance Schedule
•    Daily: backup database, monitor logs.
•    Weekly: verify backups, review storage.
•    Monthly: optimize database and audit accounts.
•    Quarterly: disaster recovery test.
•    Yearly: full security and performance review.


10. Conclusion
Consistent preventive maintenance ensures that AccredX remains secure, reliable,
and capable of supporting accreditation activities. Administrators should
document every maintenance activity and review procedures regularly.
