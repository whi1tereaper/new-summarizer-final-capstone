   Audit Trail    –  a feature of the system that
records every activity or modification made to
documents, helping to track changes and ensure
accountability.

   Document Management – a structured method for
handling documents, including creation, storage,
retrieval, and disposal.

   Electronic Records Management System (ERMS)
– a software-based system designed to manage
records in digital format, automate document
workflows,   and   enhance   security   and
accessibility.

   File Retrieval – The process of locating and
accessing   stored   documents   quickly   and
efficiently.
   ISO/IEC  25010:2011        –    an  international
standard used to evaluate software quality based
on  criteria  like  usability,  reliability,
maintainability, and performance.

   Record Management – the systematic control of
records from creation through maintenance to
disposal. It ensures records are accurate,
secure, and accessible when needed.
