Scope and Delimitation

  This  project  focuses  on  developing  and
implementing a record management and archiving
system for NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus. The
system is intended to handle academic and
administrative documents by offering a single
digital platform for storing, organizing, and
retrieving data. It is specifically designed to
improve   the   efficiency,   security,   and
accessibility of papers used by school personnel.
  The primary users of the system include school
administrators, staff, and faculty, who will
benefit from features such as fast document
search, version tracking, audit trails, and
role-based access. The scope also covers the
system’s role in supporting the         accreditation
process, ensuring that required documentation
is  readily  available,  complete,  and  well-
organized.
  The study is delimited to the Peñaranda Off-
Campus of NEUST and does not encompass other
branches or external institutions. It focuses
only on document management functions and does
not address other administrative operations
such as financial systems, student grading, or
enrollment. Additionally, this study is limited
to the system’s design, development, and basic
functional testing.
