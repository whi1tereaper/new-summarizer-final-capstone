   •  Compliance Report Generation: Users can
      generate accreditation reports summarizing
      compliance status across all Areas. These
      reports can be downloaded and shared with
      accreditation teams or external evaluators.

   •  Reduced Duplication and Errors: Version
      control and centralized storage eliminate
      the problem of multiple conflicting copies
      of  the  same  document,  ensuring  that
      everyone accesses the most current and
      accurate files.

   •  Streamlined  Workflows:  The  structured
      folder hierarchy, combined with role-based
      access, ensures that the right people have
      access to the right documents at the right
      time, reducing coordination overhead.

accreditation document management process from
a fragmented, manual effort into an organized,
automated, and auditable workflow, directly
answering how the system improves retrieval,
security, and efficiency.
