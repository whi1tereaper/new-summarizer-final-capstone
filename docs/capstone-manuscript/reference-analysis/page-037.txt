Definition of Terms

   Accreditation – a formal recognition that an
institution meets certain standards of quality.
In the context of NEUST, it involves reviewing
documents to assess academic and administrative
practices.

   Archiving   –  the process of storing records
and documents, either digitally or physically,
for long-term preservation and future reference.
