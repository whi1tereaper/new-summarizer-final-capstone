   4.2   Design Stage

   After the initial study of the researchers,
they now proceed to the Design Stage. Here,
diagrams are presented as a guide to the
development of the system. These tools became
essential   for   creating   the   system's
functionalities,   front-end   and   back-end
interfaces, and database. The visual diagrams
included in this stage are the Use Case Diagram,
Entity    Relationship    Diagram,            Database
Normalization, Data Dictionary, and          Data Flow
Diagram.
