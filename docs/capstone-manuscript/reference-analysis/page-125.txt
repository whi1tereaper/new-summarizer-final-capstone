Conclusion
  Based on the findings of the study, the
following conclusions were drawn:

     1.  The existing record management system
  at NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus was inadequate
  for  the  institution's  operational  and
  accreditation needs. The reliance on Google
  Drive without structured organization, proper
  categorization, checklist functionality, or
  centralized  control  created  significant
  inefficiencies,    security    risks,    and
  difficulties  in  document  retrieval  and
  accountability.

     2.      The Agile methodology and SDLC
  framework proved effective        in developing a
  comprehensive    and    responsive    record
  management system. The iterative development
  approach allowed for continuous refinement
  based on stakeholder feedback, ensuring the
  final   product   aligned   closely   with
  institutional    requirements    and    user
  expectations.
     3.     AccredX successfully addresses the
  identified     problems            through     its
  implementation   of   essential   features
