   After the system prototype was developed, the
researchers prepared the evaluation instruments
and  secured  permission  from  the  school
administration  to  conduct  the  study.  The
questionnaires were then distributed to the
identified respondents. IT consultants received
digital copies of the evaluation forms, while
end-users were provided with both printed and
online versions for convenience.

   Respondents  were  given  adequate  time  to
explore   the   system   and   assess   its
functionalities    before    completing    the
questionnaires. The researchers also provided
demonstrations and guidance, especially for the
end-users, to ensure that they could navigate
and evaluate the system properly.

   Once  the  responses  were  collected,  the
researchers consolidated and analyzed the data
to  determine  the  system’s  effectiveness
according to the specified software quality
standards. The feedback was used to validate the
strengths of the system and identify              areas
requiring further refinement.
