          THE PROBLEM AND ITS BACKGROUND

Introduction

  In  today's  digital  age,  proper  document
management and archiving are critical to the
efficiency,  security,  and  compliance  of
organizational operations. Records management
is the systematic control of records from
creation to disposal, ensuring that critical
information is available, protected, and kept
(Dearstyne, 2020; Bailey, 2021).

 As technology has advanced, organizations have
switched from conventional manual procedures to
electronic    records    management    systems
(ERMS), that automate classification, retrieval,
and storage processes. These modern systems help
reduce manual effort, improve security through
encryption and access control, and support
compliance   with   legal   and   regulatory
requirements (Smith & Johnson, 2020; Patterson,
2022).
