J. Profile Page























   •   In this page, the user can see their Username,
       Email and Profile Picture.
   •   You can set a profile picture by clicking the
       “Choose a new image” and clicking “Update Image”.
   •   You can change/update your email address by
       entering a new email in the textbox and clicking
       “Update Email”
   •   You can also change your password to a new one by
       entering your current password, entering a new
       password, and typing that same new password to
       confirm the changes, then click on “Update
       Password”.
