Significance of the Study

   The significance of this study lies in its
potential to address the challenges of managing
and  organizing  documents  within  the  NEUST
Peñaranda Off-Campus. By introducing a Record
Management   and   Archiving   System,   the
researchers aim to enhance the efficiency,
security, and accessibility of school records.

   The school: The system allows for centralized
control  and  management  of  academic  and
administrative documents. It improves the way
records are stored, retrieved, and maintained—
supporting institutional operations, especially
during evaluations and audits. With features
like audit trails and role-based access, the
system also promotes accountability and data
integrity.

   Staff and Teachers:      The system reduces the
time and effort required for manual filing and
record  searching.  With        advanced     search    &
filtering    functions, folder checklists, and
version control, staff can perform their tasks
more efficiently. The ability to access files
