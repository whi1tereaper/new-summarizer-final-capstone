   The second group is composed of twenty-three
(23) end-users, distributed as follows: three
(3) administrative staff, five (5) teachers, and
fifteen (15) assistants. These respondents were
selected based on their direct involvement in
the academic and administrative operations of
NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus. Their role is to
evaluate the developed system in terms of
usability,   functional   suitability,   and
performance   efficiency,   reflecting   its
applicability  in  the  actual  institutional
setting.

   This purposive selection ensured that the
system was evaluated both from a technical
perspective (IT     consultants) and a practical
user perspective (end-users).

Research Instruments

   The primary research instrument used in this
study   was   a   structured   questionnaire
distributed in both printed and digital (Google
Forms) formats. The questionnaire was designed
to gather quantitative and qualitative feedback
from respondents regarding the developed system.
