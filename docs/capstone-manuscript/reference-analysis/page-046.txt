Research Respondents

   The researchers identify two (2) sets of
respondents involved in this study. These are:
(1) the IT    consultants    who will evaluate the
technical aspects of the developed system based
on software quality standards; and (2) the end-
users who will evaluate the usability and
functionality of the developed system.

   The first set of respondents consists of ten
(10) IT   consultants, selected based on their
expertise in software development and system
analysis. Their role is to assess the technical
and functional quality of the system.

   The second set of respondents consists of
twenty-three (23) end-users, composed of three
(3) administrative staff, five (5) teachers, and
fifteen (15)    assistants. These end-users will
provide feedback based on their experience in
using   the   system   in   an   academic   or
administrative setting.
