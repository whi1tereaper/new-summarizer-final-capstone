25010:2011 standards, and twenty-three (23)

across  all  criteria  including  functional
suitability, security, performance efficiency,

portability.  End-users  similarly  gave  the
system an overall weighted mean of 3.49 (Very
Satisfactory), with particularly high marks in
usability,   functional   correctness,   and
performance efficiency.
