   The  5.0 Archive Management        process handles
inactive, completed, or historical records.
Documents that are no longer actively used may
be transferred to the archive while remaining
accessible for future reference, audits, and
accreditation purposes.
   Lastly,   6.0 Report Generation enables users
to generate reports based on stored records and
system  activities.  The  generated  reports
provide  summaries  and  documentation  that
support   decision-making,   monitoring,   and
accreditation compliance requirements.
   Overall, the Level 1 DFD demonstrates how
AccredX manages user access, maintains records,
archives documents, and generates reports to
improve  the  efficiency,  organization,  and
accessibility     of     accreditation-related
information within NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus.
