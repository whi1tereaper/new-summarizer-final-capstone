   Across sectors, digital archiving systems
consistently  report  better retrieval  speed,
storage    efficiency,    and    operational
control than manual or paper-based approaches
(Yulianti et al., 2025; Erin et al., 2025;
Pratiwi et al., 2025). Manual systems are
repeatedly  associated  with  slow  retrieval,
misplaced  files,  redundancy,  damaged  paper
records,  and  higher  administrative  burden
(Justina et al., 2022; Kustiningsih et al.,
2026).  Electronic  archivation  also  reduces
physical storage needs and can cut storage costs
substantially  through  automation  and  cloud
technologies,   with   one   recent   study
estimating 40–60% lower storage costs (Moaiyad,
2025).  In  addition,  centralized  digital
repositories support faster decision-making,
smoother workflows, and better continuity after
disruptive events such as fires or floods
(Savchenko et al., 2024; Benmakhlouf & Chouaou,
2024; Kustiningsih et al., 2026).
