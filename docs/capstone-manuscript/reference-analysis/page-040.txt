Research Design
   The research design serves as a structured
framework  that  guides  the  selection  and
application   of   appropriate   methods   and
strategies in the development of the study. This
study adopts a developmental research design,
which emphasizes the iterative creation              and
refinement of a functional prototype of              the
AccredX: Record Management and Archiving System.
The approach centers on continuous feedback from
end-users  and  stakeholders  to  inform  each
development cycle, ensuring that the system
evolves  in  response  to  actual  needs  and
practical challenges.

   To structure the development process, the
study applies the Software Development Life
Cycle (SDLC), particularly utilizing the Agile
Methodology. Agile is well-suited for dynamic
and  user-centered  systems,  as  it  promotes
adaptability,   close   collaboration,               and
incremental delivery of functional components.
