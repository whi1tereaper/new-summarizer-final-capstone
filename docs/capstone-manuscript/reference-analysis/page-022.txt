security  of  sensitive  academic  and  office
documents  with  access  control  and              user
authentication. Furthermore, the system is to
be  evaluated     based  on     ISO/IEC  25010:2011
software quality standards, with a focus on
usability,   performance   efficiency,   and
maintainability.

  This study makes a valuable contribution by
giving an innovative yet practical solution to
academic institutions' pressing documentation
and  preservation  difficulties.  The  project
provides    ongoing    quality    assurance,
institutional   accreditation,   and   general
administrative improvement through the use of
digital  technologies  and  organized  system
development. The solution serves as a scalable
structure for other educational institutions
looking to improve their records management
procedures while maintaining long-term data
integrity.
