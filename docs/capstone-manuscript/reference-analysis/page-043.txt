minor updates, bug fixes, and enhancements are
implemented. User feedback and evolving
operational requirements drive further
iterations to ensure the system remains
responsive and effective.

   The Agile approach reflects a shift from
rigid, traditional models toward a more
flexible, collaborative, and user-focused
development paradigm. By breaking the project
into smaller, time-bound iterations or
"sprints," the methodology allows for rapid
feedback cycles, increased user involvement,
and the delivery of high-quality, value-driven
outcomes.
