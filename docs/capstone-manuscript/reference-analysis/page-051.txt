Data Analysis Technique

   After  gathering  the  required  data,  the
researchers  employed  both  descriptive  and
statistical techniques to analyze and interpret
the results of the system evaluation.                For
Statement of the Problem (SOP) numbers 1 and 2,
the researchers applied qualitative description
and narrative analysis to present the existing
challenges in records management and to describe
the development process of the proposed system.
For SOP numbers 3, 4, 5, and 6, the researchers
utilized frequency distribution and weighted
mean to summarize and interpret the feedback of
the respondents. The IT        consultants    evaluated
the system in terms of functional suitability,
performance     efficiency,             compatibility,
usability,    reliability,            security       and
portability, while the end-users assessed it
mainly in terms of         functional suitability,
performance efficiency and usability.
