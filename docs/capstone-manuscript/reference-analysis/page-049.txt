   For the IT     consultants, the questionnaire
covered  the  following  criteria:  functional
suitability, performance efficiency, security,
usability, maintainability, and reliability.

   For the end-users, the questionnaire focused
on user-centered criteria such as functional
suitability,   usability,   and   performance
efficiency, aligned with their experience in
using the system for record management and
archiving tasks.

   The questionnaire consisted of a 5-point
Likert scale ranging from “Strongly Disagree”
to “Strongly Agree,” supplemented with open-
ended questions to allow respondents to provide
additional  insights  and  suggestions  for
improvement.
