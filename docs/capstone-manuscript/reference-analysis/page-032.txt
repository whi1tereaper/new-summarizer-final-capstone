   Statement of the Problem

   NEUST Peñaranda Off-Campus faces challenges
in managing and archiving important documents.
The current manual or semi-digital record-
keeping process leads to inefficiencies in file
organization, retrieval delays, security risks,
and tracking document history. These challenges
reduce the overall efficiency of administrative
tasks and accreditation processes, resulting in
misplaced files, inconsistent record updates,
and time-consuming searches.
